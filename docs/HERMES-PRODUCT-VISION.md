# Hermes Product Vision

Hermes begins as Nitin's intelligent WhatsApp personal assistant.

It should evolve into:

1. a trusted personal assistant
2. an executive chief of staff and project manager
3. an execution brain coordinating approved AI agents and systems
4. a team operating system for Om Vidya Group
5. a commercial multi-tenant product for other business owners

Primary optimisation goals:

- out-of-the-box thinking
- problem solving
- systems integration
- project management
- data-driven decisions
- outcome focus
- measurable progress
- systems-first execution
- remembering commitments and facts the user may forget
- balancing business, investment and personal priorities

Long-term morning experience:

- relevant news
- investment overview
- top three outcomes for the day
- meetings
- expected meeting outcomes
- recommended next steps
- work-life balance
- holiday planning

Current status: these long-term connectors and live data sources are future
capabilities. Hermes does not currently have Gmail, Google Calendar, ClickUp,
news, investment or travel/holiday connectors.
